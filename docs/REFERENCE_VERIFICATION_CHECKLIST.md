# Reference Verification Checklist

Before submitting the IEEE paper, verify every citation against Google Scholar, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Springer, ScienceDirect, or the publisher site. Do not keep generated or memory-based references unless the exact title, authors, venue, year, DOI, and page/article number can be confirmed.

## Safer replacement areas

- Informal transit digitisation and GTFS-like mapping: Digital Matatus, paratransit mapping, informal transport data standards.
- Multimodal route planning: transit routing, last-mile routing, paratransit accessibility.
- LLM reliability: hallucination, retrieval grounding, structured output validation.
- Passenger safety systems: emergency alerts, location sharing, women-safety mobile applications.
- ALPR: automatic license plate recognition under Indian traffic and plate conditions.
- Privacy governance: India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and urban mobility data privacy.

## Submission rule

If a reference cannot be verified, remove it. A weaker but real citation is better than a polished citation that does not exist.
